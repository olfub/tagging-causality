

# Identifying Root Variables (Unaffected by Others)

I need to determine which variables are **not caused** by any other variable in this set. Let me reason through each:

## Step-by-step Analysis

| Variable | Could it be caused by other variables in the set? | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Age (a)** | **No** | Age is a biological/temporal trait determined by birth. No other variable here (sex, education, occupation, residence, travel) causes one's age. |
| **Sex (s)** | **No** | Sex is a biological characteristic determined at birth. It is not caused by any other variable in this set. |
| **Education (e)** | **Yes** | Influenced by age (accumulation over time), sex (historical access differences), and possibly residence (urban/rural access). |
| **Occupation (o)** | **Yes** | Influenced by education, age, sex, and residence. |
| **Residence (r)** | **Yes** | Can be influenced by occupation, education, and age (e.g., migration for work/school). |
| **Travel (t)** | **Yes** | Influenced by age, sex, occupation, residence, and education (a downstream variable). |

## Conclusion

**Age (a)** and **Sex (s)** are the only variables that are **exogenous** — they are not affected by any other variables in this set. They serve as root nodes in the causal graph, driving downstream effects on education, occupation, residence, and travel.
