

# Identifying Variables Caused by `birthasphyxia`

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

**Understanding `birthasphyxia`:** This is a lack of oxygen to the blood during the infant's birth. It is specified as an exogenous variable (not affected by any other variable), so it acts as a root cause in this network.

**Evaluating each variable:**

- **`disease` (infant methemoglobinemia):** Birth asphyxia is a perinatal risk factor that can directly influence the disease state of the infant. In neonatal diagnostic networks, birth asphyxia is a recognized direct contributor to the underlying disease/condition. ✅

- **`sick`, `cardiacmixing`, `lungparench`, `ductflow`, etc.:** These are pathophysiological states and clinical signs that arise as consequences of the *disease* itself, not directly from birth asphyxia. Birth asphyxia influences these only *indirectly* through `disease`.

- **`age`:** The age at presentation is influenced by the disease characteristics, not directly by birth asphyxia.

- **Observation/report variables (`lvhreport`, `co2report`, `xrayreport`, `gruntingreport`):** These are downstream clinical observations, not directly caused by birth asphyxia.

**Conclusion:** `birthasphyxia` directly causes only `disease`, which then serves as the mediator for all downstream pathophysiological and clinical variables.
