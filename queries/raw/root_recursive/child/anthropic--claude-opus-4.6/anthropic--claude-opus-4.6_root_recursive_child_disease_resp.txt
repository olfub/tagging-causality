

# Identifying Variables Caused by Disease

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

Looking at this network (which resembles the well-known CHILD Bayesian network for infant cardiac diseases), I need to identify which variables are direct effects of `disease` (infant methemoglobinemia).

**Pathophysiological effects of the disease:**
- **`ductflow`**: The disease directly affects blood flow across the ductus arteriosus.
- **`cardiacmixing`**: The disease directly determines the degree of mixing of oxygenated and deoxygenated blood.
- **`lungparench`**: The disease directly affects the state of the lung blood vessels.
- **`lungflow`**: The disease directly determines blood flow patterns in the lungs.
- **`lvh`**: The disease directly causes left ventricular hypertrophy as a cardiac consequence.

**Clinical presentation effects:**
- **`sick`**: The disease directly determines whether the infant presents as ill.
- **`age`**: The disease type directly influences the age at which the infant presents.

**Variables NOT directly caused by disease** (they are further downstream):
- `hypdistrib`, `hypoxiaino2` → caused by `cardiacmixing`/`ductflow`/`lungparench`
- `co2` → caused by `lungparench`
- `lowerbodyo2`, `ruqo2` → caused by `hypdistrib`/`hypoxiaino2`
- `grunting` → caused by `sick`
- Report variables → caused by their respective measurements
- `birthasphyxia` → root cause (upstream)
