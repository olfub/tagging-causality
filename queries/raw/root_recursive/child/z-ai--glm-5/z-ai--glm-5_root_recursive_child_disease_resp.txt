# Step-by-step analysis of variables caused by disease

I need to identify which variables are caused by infant methemoglobinemia (the disease) based on the causal relationships provided and medical knowledge about this condition.

First, let's understand what infant methemoglobinemia does: it causes hemoglobin to be unable to carry oxygen effectively, leading to tissue hypoxia even when breathing oxygen.

## Analyzing each potential variable:

1. **birthasphyxia**: Lack of oxygen during birth. While methemoglobinemia affects oxygen carrying capacity, birth asphyxia typically results from problems during delivery rather than the blood disorder itself. This is not directly caused by the disease.

2. **hypoxiaino2**: Hypoxia when breathing oxygen. This is a direct result of methemoglobinemia because the blood cannot effectively carry oxygen regardless of oxygen availability. **Caused by disease.**

3. **hypdistrib**: Low oxygen areas equally distributed around the body. Since methemoglobinemia affects blood globally, it would cause uniformly distributed hypoxia. **Caused by disease.**

4. **lowerbodyo2**: Level of oxygen in the lower body. Directly affected by the blood's impaired oxygen carrying capacity. **Caused by disease.**

5. **ruqo2**: Level of oxygen in the right upper quadriceps. Directly affected by the blood's impaired oxygen carrying capacity. **Caused by disease.**

6. **sick**: Presence of illness. Having methemoglobinemia directly means the infant is sick. **Caused by disease.**

7. **grunting**: Grunting in infants. This is a compensatory response to hypoxia, not directly caused by the disease.

8. **co2**: Level of CO2 in the body. This would be affected by respiratory changes (like grunting), which are indirect effects of the disease.

9. **cardiacmixing**: Mixing of blood. This might be a compensatory mechanism, not directly caused by the disease.

10. **lvh**: Left ventricular hypertrophy. This would be a long-term adaptation to chronic hypoxia, not a direct effect.

11. **ductflow**: Already specified as being caused by age, not disease.

12. **lungflow**: Already specified as being caused by age, not disease.

The remaining variables are reports or measurements, not directly caused by the disease.

<Answer>hypoxiaino2, hypdistrib, lowerbodyo2, ruqo2, sick</Answer>
