**Reasoning step by step:**

**lvfailure** is defined as "dysfunction of the left ventricle causing insufficient delivery of blood to vital body organs."

When the left ventricle fails:
1. **lvedvolume** (left ventricular end-diastolic volume): Blood pools in the left ventricle because it can't pump efficiently, so this increases.

2. **strokevolume**: The volume of blood pumped out per contraction decreases because the ventricle is dysfunctional.

3. **co** (cardiac output): Cardiac output = stroke volume × heart rate. Since stroke volume decreases, CO decreases.

4. **bp** (blood pressure): With reduced cardiac output, blood pressure drops.

5. **pcwp** (pulmonary capillary wedge pressure): Blood backs up from the failing left ventricle into the pulmonary circulation, increasing this pressure.

However, **cvp** (central venous pressure) measures pressure on the right side of the heart (vena cava). This is more directly related to hypovolemia and right heart function, not directly caused by left ventricular failure.
