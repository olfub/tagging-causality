

# Variables Caused by TPR

Looking at this problem, I need to trace the causal effects of **tpr** through the network.

## Identifying TPR's Direct Effects

Based on the ALARM network structure (which this problem represents), **tpr** (total peripheral resistance) directly causes:
- **catechol** (TPR influences catecholamine release)
- **bp** (TPR directly affects blood pressure, along with cardiac output)

## Tracing Downstream Effects

**Through catechol:**
- catechol → **hr** (catecholamines affect heart rate)
- hr → **co** (heart rate affects cardiac output)
- hr → **hrekg** (heart rate appears on EKG monitor)
- hr → **hrsat** (heart rate affects oxygen saturation readings)
- co → **bp** (cardiac output affects blood pressure — already captured)
- bp → **hrbp** (blood pressure feeds into HR/BP ratio)

**Through bp directly:**
- bp → **hrbp** (already captured)

## Verification

This matches the pattern seen with **insuffanesth**, which also causes catechol as its first downstream effect and shares the exact same set of descendants: {catechol, hr, hrekg, hrsat, co, bp, hrbp}.

<Answer>catechol, hr, hrekg, hrsat, co, bp, hrbp</Answer>
