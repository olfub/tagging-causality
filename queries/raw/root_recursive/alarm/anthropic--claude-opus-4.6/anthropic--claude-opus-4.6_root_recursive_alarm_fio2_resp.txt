

# Variables Caused by `fio2`

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

Looking at the causal structure, I need to trace all downstream effects of `fio2`. While `fio2` is listed as a root variable, examining the standard causal model (ALARM network) this problem is based on, `fio2` (fraction of inspired oxygen) directly causes:

**Step 1: Direct effect**
- `fio2` → **`pvsat`** (oxygen concentration in inspired gas affects oxygen saturation in the pulmonary artery)

**Step 2: Trace through `pvsat`**
- `pvsat` → **`sao2`** (pulmonary oxygen saturation affects arterial oxygen saturation)

**Step 3: Trace through `sao2`**
- `sao2` → **`catechol`** (low oxygen saturation triggers catecholamine release)

**Step 4: Trace through `catechol`**
- `catechol` → **`hr`** (catecholamines affect heart rate)

**Step 5: Trace through `hr`**
- `hr` → **`co`** (heart rate affects cardiac output)
- `hr` → **`hrekg`** (heart rate is displayed on the EKG monitor)
- `hr` → **`hrsat`** (heart rate affects the saturation monitor reading)

**Step 6: Trace through `co`**
- `co` → **`bp`** (cardiac output affects blood pressure)

**Step 7: Trace through `hr` and `bp`**
- `hr`, `bp` → **`hrbp`** (both feed into the heart rate/blood pressure ratio)

<Answer>pvsat, sao2, catechol, hr, co, hrekg, hrsat, bp, hrbp</Answer>
