

# Variables Caused by `lvfailure`

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

**What does `lvfailure` represent?**
Left ventricular failure — the left ventricle cannot pump blood effectively.

**What physiological quantities would be directly affected?**

1. **`lvedvolume` (LV end-diastolic volume):** When the left ventricle fails, it cannot eject blood properly. This causes blood to accumulate in the ventricle, directly increasing the end-diastolic volume. → **Caused by `lvfailure`** ✓

2. **`strokevolume` (stroke volume):** A failing left ventricle directly reduces the volume of blood pumped per contraction. → **Caused by `lvfailure`** ✓

**Ruling out other variables as direct effects of `lvfailure`:**
- Variables like `co`, `bp`, `pcwp`, `pap`, etc. are downstream consequences but are mediated through `strokevolume` and `lvedvolume` — they are not *directly* caused by `lvfailure`.
- Variables like `history`, `hypovolemia`, `anaphylaxis`, etc. are listed as exogenous (not affected by any variable), so they cannot be caused by `lvfailure`.
- Ventilation-related and monitoring-related variables have no direct physiological link to LV failure itself.

<Answer>lvedvolume, strokevolume</Answer>
