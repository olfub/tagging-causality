

# Variables Caused by Hypovolemia

## Step-by-step reasoning:

1. **Hypovolemia** is defined as a condition where the body loses fluid (blood or water), meaning there is reduced circulating blood volume.

2. From the problem, we know hypovolemia is a **root node** (not caused by any other variable), so it can only be a cause of other variables.

3. Looking at the medical/physiological relationships in this network (the ALARM network):

   - **lvedvolume** (left ventricular end-diastolic volume): With less circulating blood due to hypovolemia, there is less venous return, meaning less blood fills the left ventricle before contraction. → **Hypovolemia causes lvedvolume.**

   - **strokevolume** (volume of blood pumped per contraction): With reduced filling of the left ventricle (reduced preload), the heart pumps out less blood per beat (Frank-Starling mechanism). → **Hypovolemia causes strokevolume.**

4. This mirrors the relationship already given for lvfailure (which also causes lvedvolume and strokevolume), which makes sense since both lvfailure and hypovolemia are distinct pathological causes that reduce effective cardiac output through their effects on ventricular volume and stroke volume.

<Answer>lvedvolume, strokevolume</Answer>
