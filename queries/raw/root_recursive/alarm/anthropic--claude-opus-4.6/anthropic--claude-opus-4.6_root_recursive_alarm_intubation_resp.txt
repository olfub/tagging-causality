

# Variables Caused by Intubation

Looking at the ALARM network structure, I need to identify which variables have **intubation** as a direct parent/cause.

## Step-by-step reasoning:

1. **ventlung** (lung ventilation): Intubation status (normal, esophageal, one-sided) directly affects how well the lungs are ventilated. Other parents include kinkedtube, venttube, and disconnect.

2. **ventalv** (alveolar ventilation): Intubation type directly determines whether gas exchange at the alveolar level can occur (e.g., esophageal intubation prevents alveolar ventilation). Also depends on ventlung.

3. **shunt**: Improper intubation (e.g., esophageal or one-sided) increases pulmonary shunting. Already noted that pulmembolus also causes shunt — intubation is an additional parent.

4. **press** (breathing pressure): The intubation status affects the pressure readings in the breathing circuit. Also depends on kinkedtube and venttube.

5. **minvol** (minute volume): Intubation status affects the measured minute volume (e.g., esophageal intubation would alter readings). Also depends on ventlung.

These all make medical sense: the quality/type of intubation fundamentally affects ventilation mechanics, gas exchange, shunting, airway pressures, and measured breathing volumes.

<Answer>shunt, ventlung, ventalv, press, minvol</Answer>
