

# Identifying Variables Caused by Pulmembolus

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

**Step 1:** Pulmembolus (pulmonary embolism) is identified as a root node — it is not affected by any other variable.

**Step 2:** I need to determine its direct causal effects based on medical/physiological reasoning and the structure of this causal network (which corresponds to the well-known ALARM network).

**Step 3:** A pulmonary embolus is a sudden blockage in the pulmonary arteries. Its direct physiological consequences are:

- **pap (pulmonary artery pressure):** A blockage in the pulmonary arteries directly increases the blood pressure in those arteries (pulmonary hypertension).
- **shunt:** A pulmonary embolus blocks blood flow through part of the lung, causing a mismatch where blood bypasses functional gas exchange areas, effectively creating a shunt.

**Step 4:** Other variables like sao2, pvsat, etc., may be *indirectly* affected (downstream), but the **direct** causal children of pulmembolus are pap and shunt.

<Answer>pap, shunt</Answer>
