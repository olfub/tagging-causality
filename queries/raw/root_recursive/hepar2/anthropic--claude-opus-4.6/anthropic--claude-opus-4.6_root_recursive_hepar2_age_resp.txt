

# Variables Caused by Age

Looking at this medical Bayesian network (which appears to be the HEPAR2 network for liver disease), I need to identify what conditions age directly causes.

## Step-by-step reasoning:

1. **Age is a root node** – it is not affected by any other variable.

2. **Medical knowledge about age as a risk factor:**
   - **Gallstones**: Age is a well-known risk factor (prevalence increases with age). Note: sex also causes gallstones, so gallstones has multiple parents.
   - **PBC (Primary Biliary Cirrhosis)**: Age is a risk factor for PBC. Note: sex also causes PBC.
   - **Diabetes**: Age is a major risk factor for diabetes (especially type 2).
   - **Obesity**: Age influences obesity risk (metabolic changes over time).

3. **Consistency check**: In the HEPAR2 network structure, age serves as a parent node to these variables, and both age and sex can jointly influence some of the same variables (like gallstones and PBC).

<Answer>gallstones, pbc, diabetes, obesity</Answer>
