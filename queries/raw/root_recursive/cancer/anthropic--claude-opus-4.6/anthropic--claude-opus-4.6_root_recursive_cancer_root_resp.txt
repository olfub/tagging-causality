

# Identifying Root Variables (Unaffected by Others)

Let me reason through each variable step by step:

1. **Pollution**: This is an environmental/external factor. None of the other variables (Smoker, Cancer, Xray, Dyspnoea) would *cause* pollution levels. → **Root variable**

2. **Smoker**: This is a behavioral/lifestyle choice. It is not caused by pollution, cancer, x-ray results, or dyspnoea. → **Root variable**

3. **Cancer**: Lung cancer can be *caused* by both pollution exposure and smoking. → **Affected by other variables**

4. **Xray**: An x-ray result (e.g., abnormal finding) is influenced by the presence of cancer. → **Affected by other variables**

5. **Dyspnoea**: Difficulty in breathing can be *caused* by lung cancer. → **Affected by other variables**

## Conclusion
**Pollution** and **Smoker** are the exogenous variables — they are not caused by any other variable in this set. They serve as the root nodes of the causal graph.
