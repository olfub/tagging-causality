

# Variables Caused by `drvset`

Looking at this step by step:

## Step 1: Identify the direct child of `drvset`
`drvset` (Driver Settings) along with `prtdriver` (Printer Driver) determines whether the driver functions correctly:
- **`drvok`** (Driver OK) — direct child of `drvset`

## Step 2: Trace through the GDI pipeline
- **`gdiout`** (GDI Output) — depends on `drvok` and `gdiin`
- **`prtdataout`** (Print Data Out) — depends on `gdiout` (and `prtpath`)

## Step 3: Trace downstream to printer communication
- **`pc2prt`** (PC to Printer) — depends on `prtdataout` plus connection paths (`ds_lclok`, `ds_ntok`)

## Step 4: Trace to print data and problems
Following the same downstream pattern shown in the `dsklocal` example (which also feeds into `pc2prt` through the local path):
- **`prtdata`** (Print Data) — downstream of `pc2prt`
- **`prttimeout`** (Print Timeout) — downstream of `pc2prt`/`prtdata`
- **`fllcrrptdbffr`** (Full/Corrupted Buffer) — downstream of `pc2prt`/`prtdata`
- **`problem1`** (Problem1) — downstream of `prtdata`
- **`hrglssdrtnaftrprnt`** (Hourglass Duration After Print) — downstream of `problem1`

## Verification
This mirrors the `dsklocal` pattern: `dsklocal` reaches `pc2prt` through the local connection path (`ds_lclok`, `lclok`), while `drvset` reaches `pc2prt` through the data processing pipeline (`drvok` → `gdiout` → `prtdataout`). Both share the downstream nodes from `pc2prt` onward.

<Answer>drvok, gdiout, prtdataout, pc2prt, prtdata, prttimeout, fllcrrptdbffr, problem1, hrglssdrtnaftrprnt</Answer>
