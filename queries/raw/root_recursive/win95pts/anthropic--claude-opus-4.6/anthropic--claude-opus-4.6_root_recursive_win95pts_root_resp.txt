## Reasoning Process

I need to identify variables that represent **fundamental states, hardware properties, user configurations, or document properties** — things that aren't caused by other variables in the system.

**Physical/Hardware States:** `PrtOn` (printer powered on), `PrtPaper` (paper loaded), `PrtCbl` (cable connected), `PrtMem` (printer memory), `TnrSpply` (toner supply), `DskLocal` (local disk OK), `PrtPort` (port configured), `PrtPScript` (PostScript-capable printer), `PrntrAccptsTrtyp` (printer accepts TrueType)
