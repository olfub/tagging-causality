

# Variables Caused by Socioeconomic Status

Looking at this step by step, I need to identify which variables **socioeconomic status** directly causes, considering the domain (auto insurance) and the structure of the well-known Insurance Bayesian network.

## Reasoning

**socioeconomic status** logically influences:

1. **goodstudent** – SES affects educational engagement and resources (also caused by age, as stated).
2. **riskaversion** – SES shapes attitudes toward risk (also caused by age, as stated).
3. **vehicleyear** – SES determines what year/quality of vehicle one can afford.
4. **makemodel** – SES influences whether someone owns a sports car.
5. **homebase** – SES directly determines the type of neighbourhood one lives in.
6. **antitheft** – SES affects whether one invests in anti-theft devices.

These are all variables where socioeconomic status is a direct causal factor — they relate to lifestyle choices, purchasing power, and risk attitudes shaped by one's economic standing.

<Answer>goodstudent, riskaversion, vehicleyear, makemodel, homebase, antitheft</Answer>
