

# Variables Caused by Age

Looking at this step by step, I need to consider which variables age would directly influence in an auto insurance context.

1. **goodstudent**: Age determines eligibility for "good student" status (only young drivers qualify) ✅
2. **riskaversion**: Risk tolerance changes with age (older people tend to be more risk-averse) ✅
3. **seniortrain**: Senior/additional driving training is age-dependent (offered to elderly drivers) ✅
4. **drivingskill**: Driving skill is influenced by age (experience accumulates over time, but may decline in old age) ✅
5. **medcost**: Age affects medical costs (older individuals are more vulnerable to injury and have higher treatment costs) ✅
6. **mileage**: Driving mileage patterns depend on age (working-age vs. retired, young vs. old) ✅

Other variables like `vehicleyear`, `makemodel`, `homebase`, etc., are more naturally driven by socioeconomic status and risk preferences rather than directly by age.

<Answer>goodstudent, riskaversion, seniortrain, drivingskill, medcost, mileage</Answer>
