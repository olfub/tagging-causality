

# Identifying Root Variables (Unaffected by Others)

I need to determine which variables are **not caused by any other variables** in this system. Let me reason through each variable:

## Step-by-step analysis:

1. **`asia`** (recently visited Asia): This is a background/travel factor. No disease or clinical variable in this set would *cause* someone to visit Asia. → **Root variable**

2. **`smoke`** (smoker status): This is a lifestyle/behavioral factor. None of the other variables (diseases, tests) would cause someone to smoke. → **Root variable**

3. **`tub`** (tuberculosis): Visiting Asia increases TB risk (caused by `asia`). → **Not a root**

4. **`lung`** (lung cancer): Smoking causes lung cancer (caused by `smoke`). → **Not a root**

5. **`bronc`** (bronchitis): Smoking causes bronchitis (caused by `smoke`). → **Not a root**

6. **`either`** (TB or lung cancer): This is a logical/deterministic node caused by `tub` and `lung`. → **Not a root**

7. **`xray`** (positive chest X-ray): An abnormal X-ray is caused by having TB or lung cancer (`either`). → **Not a root**

8. **`dysp`** (dyspnoea): Shortness of breath is caused by `either` (TB/lung cancer) and `bronc`. → **Not a root**

## Summary of causal flow:
- `asia` → `tub` → `either` → `xray`, `dysp`
- `smoke` → `lung` → `either`
- `smoke` → `bronc` → `dysp`

The two variables with **no incoming causal edges** are the background factors.

<Answer>asia, smoke</Answer>
