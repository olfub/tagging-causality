**Step 2: Analyze the Clinical Workflow**
*   Invasive mechanical ventilation requires a secure airway to deliver pressurized air effectively into the lungs.
*   The standard procedure is:
    1.  The patient experiences respiratory failure.
    2.  The medical team performs **INTUBATION** to secure the airway.
    3.  The patient is connected to a mechanical ventilator.
    4.  The ventilator delivers breaths, establishing **VENTALV** (Alveolar Ventilation).

*   **INTUBATION $\rightarrow$ VENTALV**: The procedure of intubation is a prerequisite for invasive mechanical alveolar ventilation. Without intubation, the specific mechanical ventilation parameters recorded as `VENTALV` in an ICU setting cannot be established or measured in the same way. The intervention (intubation) enables the state (ventilation).

The recorded parameter does not cause the procedure; rather, the procedure allows the parameter to exist as a controlled variable.

**Step 4: Conclusion**
Based on the temporal order and clinical dependency, the act of intubation causes the establishment of mechanical alveolar ventilation. Therefore, `INTUBATION` is the cause, and `VENTALV` is the effect.
