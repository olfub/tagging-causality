

# Causal Reasoning: MvmtFeatures vs. RHRatio

## Step-by-step Analysis

**Understanding the variables:**
- **RHRatio** (Relative Humidity Ratio): An atmospheric/environmental parameter describing the moisture profile of the atmosphere (e.g., ratio of relative humidity at different vertical levels). This is typically a **pre-storm environmental condition**.
- **MvmtFeatures** (Movement Features): Characteristics related to storm motion (speed, direction, propagation). These are **storm behavior characteristics**.

**Causal reasoning in hail forecasting:**
1. In meteorological causal chains, **environmental conditions** (thermodynamic and kinematic profiles) are generally the **upstream drivers** of storm characteristics and behavior.
2. The moisture environment (captured by RHRatio) influences atmospheric instability, wind profiles, and storm dynamics — all of which affect how a storm moves.
3. Storm movement features are an **outcome** shaped by the atmospheric environment, not the other way around.
4. While storms can locally modify humidity, in a forecasting causal framework, the environmental state is set *before* storm behavior manifests.

**Conclusion:** The environmental moisture parameter (RHRatio) is the cause, and storm movement behavior (MvmtFeatures) is the effect.
