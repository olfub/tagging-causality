To determine the most likely cause-and-effect relationship, let's analyze the medical pathology and etiology of the terms involved.

**Step 1: Define the terms.**
*   **Alcohol (specifically Ethanol):** A psychoactive substance found in drinks that is metabolized by the liver. Chronic, excessive consumption is toxic to body tissues.
*   **Cirrhosis:** A late stage of scarring (fibrosis) of the liver caused by many forms of liver diseases and conditions. It represents a structural change in the organ where healthy liver tissue is replaced by scar tissue, impairing liver function.

**Step 2: Analyze Relationship A (Cirrhosis causes alcohol).**
*   This statement suggests that having liver scarring (cirrhosis) results in the production or presence of alcohol.
*   While the body can produce minute amounts of endogenous alcohol via gut fermentation, cirrhosis itself does not create alcohol as a product.
*   Furthermore, "alcohol" in this context refers to the consumption of alcoholic beverages. A liver condition cannot retroactively cause the consumption of a substance. Therefore, this causal direction is biologically and logically incorrect.

**Step 3: Analyze Relationship B (Alcohol causes Cirrhosis).**
*   This statement suggests that the consumption of alcohol leads to liver scarring.
*   **Mechanism:** When alcohol is metabolized in the liver, it produces toxic byproducts like acetaldehyde. Chronic exposure to these toxins causes inflammation (alcoholic hepatitis), fatty liver changes (steatosis), and eventually fibrosis (scarring). Over time, this extensive scarring is classified as cirrhosis.
*   **Medical Consensus:** Alcoholic liver disease is one of the leading causes of cirrhosis worldwide. There is a well-established, dose-dependent relationship between long-term heavy alcohol use and the development of cirrhosis.

**Step 4: Conclusion.**
Relationship B is supported by decades of medical research and established pathophysiology. Relationship A is physically impossible in the context of causation.

<Answer>B</Answer>
