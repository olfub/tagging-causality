# Analyzing the Cause-and-Effect Relationship

Let me analyze both potential causal relationships carefully:

## Understanding RHepatitis
RHepatitis (likely referring to Rheumatic Hepatitis) is an inflammatory condition of the liver associated with rheumatic diseases. Like other forms of hepatitis, it involves liver inflammation and impaired liver function.

## Option A: RHepatitis causes fatigue
- Liver diseases, including various forms of hepatitis, are well-documented to cause fatigue as a primary symptom
- When the liver is inflamed, its ability to perform critical functions is compromised
- This leads to metabolic disturbances, toxin accumulation, and altered energy metabolism
- Fatigue is recognized as one of the most common and debilitating symptoms of liver disease in clinical practice

## Option B: Fatigue causes RHepatitis
- Fatigue is a non-specific symptom associated with many conditions
- There is no established biological mechanism by which fatigue alone would trigger liver inflammation
- Fatigue lacks the specificity to cause a particular pathological condition like RHepatitis
- In medical pathophysiology, symptoms generally result from diseases rather than causing them

## Conclusion
The relationship in Option A follows the established pattern where a disease causes symptoms. Liver dysfunction directly impacts energy metabolism and results in fatigue. Option B contradicts our understanding of disease processes.

<Answer>A</Answer>
