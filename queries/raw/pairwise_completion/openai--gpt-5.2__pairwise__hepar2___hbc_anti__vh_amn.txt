2) **Biological mechanism / temporal ordering**
- In HBV infection (a form of viral hepatitis), the body mounts an immune response.
- That immune response includes production of **anti-HBc**.  
  So the typical sequence is: **viral hepatitis (HBV infection) → immune activation → anti-HBc positivity**.

3) **Plausibility of the reverse direction**
- **anti-HBc** is a *marker* of exposure/infection (and sometimes past infection); it does **not** biologically *cause* viral hepatitis. If anything, antibodies are part of protection/immune history rather than a cause of liver inflammation.

4) **Conclusion**
The more likely causal direction is **viral hepatitis leading to anti-HBc**, not the other way around.
