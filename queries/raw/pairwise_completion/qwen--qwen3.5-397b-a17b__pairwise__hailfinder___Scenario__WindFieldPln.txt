**Scenario** - In hail forecasting, a "scenario" typically refers to a forecasted weather situation or a conceptual model of what conditions might develop. It's an analytical construct used to predict outcomes.

## Step 2: Understanding Causal Direction in Meteorology

In weather forecasting:
- **Physical atmospheric conditions** (like wind fields, temperature, humidity) are the fundamental inputs
- **Forecast scenarios** are constructed based on these physical measurements and data
- You measure/observe atmospheric conditions first, then build predictive scenarios from them

## Step 3: Applying to Hail Forecasting

For hail specifically:
- Wind shear and wind field patterns are critical factors that influence storm development
- These wind conditions help determine storm structure, rotation potential, and hail formation
- Forecasters analyze wind field data to create scenarios about potential hail events
- The scenario is the *output* of analyzing wind field information, not the cause of it
