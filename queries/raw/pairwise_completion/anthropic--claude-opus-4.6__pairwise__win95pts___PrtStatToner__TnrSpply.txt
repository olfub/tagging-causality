

# Analyzing the Causal Relationship: PrtStatToner vs. TnrSpply

## Step-by-step reasoning:

1. **Identify the variables:**
   - **TnrSpply** = The actual toner supply level (the physical state of toner in the cartridge)
   - **PrtStatToner** = The printer's status report/indicator about toner (what the printer displays or reports about toner)

2. **Determine the causal direction:**
   - The *actual physical toner supply level* is what drives the printer's sensor readings, which in turn determine what the printer *reports* as its toner status.
   - The printer status display about toner is an **observation/measurement** of the underlying physical reality (the toner supply).

3. **Apply causal logic:**
   - The real-world physical quantity (TnrSpply) is the **cause**.
   - The reported/displayed status (PrtStatToner) is the **effect** (an observable indicator of the underlying state).
   - A status report doesn't change the toner level; rather, the toner level determines what status is reported.

## Conclusion:
The actual toner supply level (**TnrSpply**) causes the printer to report a particular toner status (**PrtStatToner**), not the other way around.
