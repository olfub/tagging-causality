1) **Interpret the variables**
- **CO2**: the patient’s true physiologic CO₂ level (e.g., arterial/venous CO₂ or serum bicarbonate reported as “CO2” on a BMP, depending on context).
- **CO2Report**: the **reported/measured** CO₂ value produced by a lab device/clinical documentation system.

2) **Causal mechanism**
- The patient’s underlying CO₂ status exists **first**.
- The lab/analyzer/EMR then **measures and records** that value, generating **CO2Report**.
- While the *report* can influence subsequent clinical actions (ventilation changes, etc.), it does not retroactively create the patient’s CO₂ value at the time of measurement.

3) **Direction of causation**
- **CO2 → CO2Report** is the standard “quantity → measurement/report” relationship.
