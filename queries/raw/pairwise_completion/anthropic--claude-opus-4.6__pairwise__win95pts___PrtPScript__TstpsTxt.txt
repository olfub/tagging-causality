   - **TstpsTxt** likely refers to "Test PostScript Text" — a diagnostic step involving testing PostScript text output.

2. **Consider the causal direction:**

   - Testing PostScript text is a **downstream diagnostic action** that is only relevant *because* the printer is PostScript-capable.
   - It makes sense that the printer being PostScript (PrtPScript) would **lead to / cause** the need to test PostScript text (TstpsTxt), not the other way around.

3. **Reverse direction check:**
