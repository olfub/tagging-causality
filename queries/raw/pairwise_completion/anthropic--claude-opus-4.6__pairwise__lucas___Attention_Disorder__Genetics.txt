

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

**Step 1: Understand the variables.**
- **Genetics** refers to inherited DNA and genetic makeup, determined at conception.
- **Attention_Disorder** (e.g., ADHD) is a neurodevelopmental condition affecting focus and behavior.

**Step 2: Consider the directionality of causation.**
- Genetics is a foundational biological factor established before birth. It is upstream of most phenotypic traits and disorders.
- Attention disorders like ADHD are highly heritable (~70–80% heritability), meaning genetic variation plays a major role in their development.

**Step 3: Evaluate Option A (Attention_Disorder → Genetics).**
- A behavioral/neurodevelopmental disorder cannot alter a person's inherited genetic code. This reverses the logical causal order.

**Step 4: Evaluate Option B (Genetics → Attention_Disorder).**
- Genetic variants (e.g., in dopamine receptor and transporter genes) are well-established risk factors for ADHD. This follows the standard biological causal pathway: genes → brain development → attention disorder.

**Conclusion:** Genetics is a cause/risk factor for Attention Disorder, not the other way around.
