Cirrhosis often leads to liver cell damage, causing the release of intracellular enzymes (which are proteins) into the bloodstream, such as elevated ALT and AST levels. While cirrhosis also affects synthetic protein production (e.g., decreased albumin), the leakage of enzymes is a direct cause-and-effect relationship. In contrast, high protein levels are not a recognized cause of cirrhosis; cirrhosis typically results from chronic liver injury. Thus, A is more plausible.
