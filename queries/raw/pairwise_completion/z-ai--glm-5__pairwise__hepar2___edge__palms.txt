# Analyzing the Cause-and-Effect Relationship

I need to determine which causal relationship is more plausible between "edge" and "palms" in a medical context.

## Analyzing Statement A: "edge causes palms"
- In medical terminology, this doesn't form a coherent relationship
- "Edge" is not a recognized medical cause of "palms" (referring to the hand part)
- This statement lacks logical medical causation

## Analyzing Statement B: "palms causes edge"
- This suggests the palms somehow cause an "edge" phenomenon
- In medical contexts, certain palm conditions might cause distinctive edges or borders in skin manifestations
- For example, palmoplantar pustulosis creates well-defined edges on the palms
- Other palm conditions may create distinguishable border patterns

## Conclusion
When examining these options in a medical context, statement B is more plausible. While neither statement is particularly clear as written, "palms causes edge" could refer to how certain palm conditions manifest with distinctive borders or edges in their presentation.

<Answer>B</Answer>
