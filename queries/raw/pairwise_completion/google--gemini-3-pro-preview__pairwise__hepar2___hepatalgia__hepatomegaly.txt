To determine the most likely cause-and-effect relationship, let's define the medical terms and analyze the physiological mechanisms:

1.  **Definitions:**
    *   **Hepatomegaly:** This refers to the enlargement of the liver. It is a physical sign indicating an underlying issue such as hepatitis, fatty liver disease, heart failure, or cancer.
    *   **Hepatalgia:** This refers to pain in the liver. Since the liver parenchyma (tissue) itself generally lacks pain receptors, this pain usually results from the stretching or irritation of the liver capsule (Glisson's capsule).

2.  **Mechanism Analysis:**
    *   **Does pain cause the liver to grow? (A)** Pain is a symptom, a sensory experience. While chronic stress might have physiological effects, the sensation of pain itself does not physically cause the organ to increase in size.
    *   **Does an enlarged liver cause pain? (B)** When the liver enlarges (hepatomegaly), it stretches the fibrous covering of the liver known as Glisson's capsule. This capsule is innervated and sensitive to stretching. rapid or significant enlargement exerts tension on this capsule, resulting in pain (hepatalgia).

3.  **Conclusion:** The physical enlargement of the organ is the underlying condition that leads to the symptom of pain through the stretching of the liver capsule.

Therefore, the enlargement causes the pain.

<Answer>B</Answer>
