Let's analyze the relationship between **Cirrhosis** and **spiders** in a medical context.

1.  **Define the Terms:**
    *   **Cirrhosis:** This is a late stage of scarring (fibrosis) of the liver caused by many forms of liver diseases and conditions, such as hepatitis and chronic alcoholism.
    *   **Spiders (Spider Angiomas/Spider Naevi):** These are vascular lesions consisting of a central arteriole surrounded by many smaller vessels resembling spider legs. They are cutaneous (skin) findings.

2.  **Analyze Option A (Cirrhosis causes spiders):**
    *   In patients with cirrhosis, potential complications include the inability of the liver to metabolize estrogen properly.
    *   Elevated serum estrogen levels (hyperestrogenism) result in vascular dilation.
    *   This vascular dilation manifests on the skin as spider angiomas (spiders).
    *   Therefore, the pathophysiology of cirrhosis leads to the physical sign of spider angiomas.

3.  **Analyze Option B (spiders causes Cirrhosis):**
    *   Spider angiomas are a symptom or a physical sign on the skin. They are a manifestation of an underlying issue (like pregnancy, liver disease, or nutritional deficiency).
    *   The presence of these skin lesions does not induce damage to the liver or cause scarring (which defines cirrhosis).
    *   Therefore, the skin condition does not cause the organ disease.

4.  **Conclusion:**
    *   Cirrhosis is the underlying pathology that leads to hormonal imbalances, which subsequently cause the formation of spider angiomas. Thus, the direction of causality is from the liver disease to the skin manifestation.
