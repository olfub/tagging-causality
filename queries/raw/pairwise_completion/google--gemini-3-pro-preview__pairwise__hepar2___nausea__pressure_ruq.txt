*   When organs in the RUQ (like the gallbladder or liver) are stretched or inflamed, they send visceral sensory signals that stimulate the vomiting center, resulting in the symptom of nausea.
*   This is a classic presentation: A patient develops biliary colic (pressure/pain in the RUQ), and this visceral distress triggers nausea and vomiting.
