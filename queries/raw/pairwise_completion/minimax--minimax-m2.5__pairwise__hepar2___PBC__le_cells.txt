     - Histological samples from PBC patients show bile‑duct injury, hepatocyte ballooning, and leukocyte infiltrates – all consistent with the disease *causing* changes in liver cells.

   - **B. le_cells → PBC**  
     - While immune cells (a subset of “le_cells”) are involved in the autoimmune pathogenesis of PBC, the disease is not generally described as being *caused* by a primary abnormality of liver cells themselves.

   - The conventional understanding of PBC is that the autoimmune process *leads to* liver‑cell injury and inflammation, not the other way around.  
   - Therefore, the causal relationship “PBC causes le_cells” is far more consistent with the pathophysiology of the disease.

**Conclusion:** The more likely cause‑and‑effect relationship is that PBC leads to abnormalities in liver (or inflammatory) cells.
