## Step-by-Step Analysis

### Step 1: Identify the Variables

**hbc_anti**: This refers to **Hepatitis B Core Antibody** (anti-HBc). This is an antibody produced by the immune system in response to Hepatitis B virus infection.

**vh_amn**: This likely refers to a **viral hepatitis** related measure or condition (possibly viral hepatitis activity/manifestation).

### Step 2: Understand the Biological Relationship

In immunology and infectious disease:
- **Antibodies are produced IN RESPONSE to infections/antigens**
- The immune system detects a pathogen (virus) and produces antibodies to fight it
- Antibodies are markers/effects of infection, not causes of infection

### Step 3: Determine Causal Direction

- **Viral hepatitis infection** (vh_amn) → triggers immune response → **antibody production** (hbc_anti)
- The reverse (antibodies causing viral hepatitis) does not make biological sense
- Antibodies cannot cause the viral infection itself; they are a consequence of exposure to the virus

### Step 4: Apply Medical Logic

In Hepatitis B serology:
- Presence of anti-HBc (hbc_anti) indicates past or current HBV infection
- The infection must occur FIRST for antibodies to be produced
- Therefore, the hepatitis condition causes the antibody marker, not vice versa

### Conclusion

The causal relationship follows the standard immunological principle: **infection/disease → antibody production**
