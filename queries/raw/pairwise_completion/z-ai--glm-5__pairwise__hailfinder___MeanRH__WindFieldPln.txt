# Analyzing the Cause-and-Effect Relationship Between MeanRH and WindFieldPln

To determine the most likely causal relationship between these variables, I need to consider what each represents and their physical relationships in the atmosphere.

## Understanding the Variables

- **MeanRH**: Mean Relative Humidity - measures the amount of moisture in the air relative to its capacity at a given temperature
- **WindFieldPln**: Wind Field Pattern - likely refers to wind speed, direction, and flow characteristics in a specific area

## Analyzing Possible Causal Directions

### Option A: MeanRH causes WindFieldPln
- Humidity can affect air density and pressure gradients
- However, humidity alone doesn't directly generate or determine wind field patterns
- Any influence would be indirect and relatively weak

### Option B: WindFieldPln causes MeanRH
- Wind directly transports air masses with different moisture contents
- Wind patterns can bring humid or dry air into a region, directly affecting humidity
- Wind also influences evaporation rates, which affects humidity
- This is a common and well-documented relationship in meteorology
- In hail forecasting specifically, wind fields are known to transport moisture and influence humidity patterns

## Conclusion
Based on atmospheric physics and common meteorological relationships, wind field patterns more directly and strongly influence relative humidity levels than the reverse. Wind transport mechanisms provide a clear causal pathway for changing humidity in a region.

<Answer>B</Answer>
