# Analyzing the Causal Relationship Between MeanRH and RHRatio

## Understanding the Variables

**MeanRH**: This likely refers to Mean Relative Humidity - an average measurement of relative humidity across an atmospheric layer or region.

**RHRatio**: This likely refers to Relative Humidity Ratio - a derived metric that probably represents a ratio between humidity measurements at different atmospheric levels or times.

## Causal Analysis

Let me think about the logical relationship between these variables:

1. **Physical reality of humidity**: Relative humidity is a fundamental atmospheric property that exists as a direct measurement of moisture content in the air.

2. **Nature of ratios**: Ratios are mathematical constructs derived from primary measurements. An RHRatio is likely calculated by dividing humidity values (possibly including MeanRH) from different levels or conditions.

3. **Direction of derivation**:
   - MeanRH is a primary or averaged measurement of an actual atmospheric condition
   - RHRatio is a derived metric that requires humidity values to exist first in order to be calculated

4. **Temporal sequence**: The atmospheric humidity exists and is measured before any ratio can be calculated from it.

## Conclusion

MeanRH represents a more fundamental atmospheric measurement, while RHRatio is likely a derived metric that depends on humidity values for its calculation. Therefore, it makes more sense that MeanRH would causally influence RHRatio (as one of the inputs used to calculate the ratio), rather than the reverse.
