**Step 2: Analyze the Direction of Influence**

If the physical printer is turned on (`PrtOn`), the system detects power and readiness, changing the status. If the physical printer is off, the system detects no signal and reports the status as off (`PrtStatOff`).

Changing the status indicator in the software (or observing it) does not physically turn the printer's power switch on or off. If the printer is off (`PrtOn` is false), the status reports off (`PrtStatOff` is true). If the printer is on, the status reports on. *   **Option B (`PrtStatOff` causes `PrtOn`)**: This suggests that the status reporting being "off" causes the printer to turn on (or determines its power state). This is logically incorrect; a status light or software indicator cannot cause the physical hardware to power up.
