When bile contains too much cholesterol and not enough bile salts, it can become supersaturated with cholesterol. This supersaturation can lead to the precipitation of cholesterol crystals, which can then combine to form stones. Dietary factors, including high intake of calories and fats, can influence bile composition and increase the risk of gallstones. Obesity, which is often associated with higher dietary fat intake, is a known risk factor for gallstone disease.
