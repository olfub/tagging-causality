Here is a step-by-step analysis of the relationship between Age and DrivingSkill:

**Step 1: Analyze the temporal relationship.**
Temporal precedence is a fundamental requirement for causality; the cause must happen before the effect.
*   **Age:** Age is a measure of time lived. It increases constantly and inevitably for everyone.
*   **DrivingSkill:** This is a learned ability that develops over time through practice and training.
*   **Comparison:** A person must exist (and age) before they can learn to drive. Therefore, Age precedes DrivingSkill. It is impossible for DrivingSkill to precede Age.

**Step 2: Analyze the mechanism.**
*   **Option A (Age causes DrivingSkill):** As people age, they accumulate experience. In the context of driving, starting as a novice, years of practice typically improve driving skills (up to a certain point). Additionally, biological maturation affects cognitive functions like reaction time and decision-making, which directly determine driving skill. Thus, there is a clear mechanism connecting Age to DrivingSkill.
*   **Option B (DrivingSkill causes Age):** This would imply that having high or low driving skill causes a person to become older or younger. While it is true that *very poor* driving skill (leading to fatal accidents) can prevent someone from reaching an old age (survival bias), this is a secondary selection effect rather than a primary causal law. High driving skill does not biologically make a person older.

**Step 3: Evaluate the direction of causality.**
Age is an exogenous variable (it is determined by the passage of time, independent of other variables in the model). DrivingSkill is an endogenous variable that depends on time, practice, and biology. In causal modeling, variables that measure the accumulation of time or experience (Age) cause the development of capabilities (Skill), not the other way around.

**Conclusion:**
Age is a prerequisite for acquiring experience, which is the primary determinant of DrivingSkill. While driving skill can influence *survival* to an advanced age, the primary and direct causal flow is that the passage of time (Age) allows for the development and changes in DrivingSkill.
