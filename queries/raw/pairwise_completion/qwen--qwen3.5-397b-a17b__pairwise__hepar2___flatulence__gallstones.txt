When undigested fats reach the colon, bacteria ferment them, producing excess gas.
*   **Clinical Presentation:** Patients with symptomatic gallstones often report "biliary dyspepsia," which includes symptoms such as bloating, indigestion, nausea, and increased flatulence, particularly after consuming fatty meals.
*   **Medical Consensus:** While flatulence is a non-specific symptom, it is a recognized downstream effect of biliary pathology and digestive impairment caused by gallstones.
