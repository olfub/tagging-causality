1. **Temporal order / plausibility:** Congenital heart diseases are typically present **before birth** (structural or functional cardiac abnormalities). Birth asphyxia occurs **around delivery** as a consequence of impaired oxygenation/perfusion.

2. **Mechanistic pathway (common in CHD):** Many congenital heart diseases can directly lead to **perinatal hypoxemia and poor systemic perfusion** (e.g., ductal-dependent lesions, severe outflow obstruction, transposition physiology), which can present at delivery as **low Apgar scores, cyanosis, respiratory distress, and “asphyxia-like” depression**.

3. **Reverse direction less plausible:** **Birth asphyxia** can cause multi-organ injury (brain, kidney, myocardium) and may worsen cardiopulmonary status, but it does **not** plausibly *cause* a congenital heart disease itself (the “Disease” in this clinical framing).

**Conclusion:** The more likely causal direction is that the underlying **Disease** leads to apparent **BirthAsphyxia** at or shortly after delivery.
