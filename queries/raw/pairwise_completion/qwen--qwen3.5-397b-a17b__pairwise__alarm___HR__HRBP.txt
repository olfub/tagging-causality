## Step 2: Physiological Causality Principles

In patient monitoring systems:

1. **Primary vs. Derived Metrics:** Heart rate is a **primary physiological measurement** obtained directly from ECG or pulse oximetry.

2. **Direction of Causation:** Primary measurements typically **cause/contribute to** derived metrics, not the reverse. A derived metric cannot causally influence the raw data it's calculated from.

3. **Temporal Order:** Heart rate changes occur first, and any metric incorporating HR would reflect those changes afterward.
