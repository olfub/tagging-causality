2. **What is PrtDriver?**  
   “PrtDriver” is the printer driver – the component that translates the GDI calls into the specific command language (e.g., PCL, PostScript) that the printer understands.

3. **Typical printing flow in Windows 95**  
   - An application issues GDI calls (e.g., `TextOut`, `BitBlt`).  
   - The GDI subsystem receives those calls and passes them to the appropriate printer driver.  
   - The driver then creates the print job and sends it to the spooler/printer.

   The GDI output (the calls from the application) *triggers* the printer driver; the driver does not spontaneously generate GDI calls. Hence the driver is *caused* by the GDI output, not the other way around.
