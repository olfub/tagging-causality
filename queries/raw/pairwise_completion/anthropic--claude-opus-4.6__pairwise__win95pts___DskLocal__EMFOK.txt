

# Analyzing the Causal Relationship: DskLocal and EMFOK

## Understanding the Variables

In the context of Windows 95 printer troubleshooting:

- **DskLocal**: Refers to whether there is sufficient **local disk space** available.
- **EMFOK**: Refers to whether **EMF (Enhanced Metafile) spooling** is functioning correctly.

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

1. **EMF spooling** in Windows 95 works by writing temporary enhanced metafile data to the local disk before sending it to the printer.
2. This process **requires available local disk space** to write those temporary spool files.
3. If local disk space is insufficient (**DskLocal = false**), the EMF spooling process will **fail** (**EMFOK = false**).
4. The availability of disk space is a **precondition/resource** that determines whether EMF spooling can succeed — not the other way around.
5. EMF spooling succeeding or failing does not determine whether you have local disk space.

## Conclusion

The causal direction flows from the **resource condition** (disk space) to the **process outcome** (EMF spool success):

**DskLocal → EMFOK**
