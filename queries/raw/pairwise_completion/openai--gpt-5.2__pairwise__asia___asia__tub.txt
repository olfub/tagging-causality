1. **Check what each term represents**
   - **Asia** is a geographic region (a place).
   - **Tuberculosis (TB)** is an infectious disease caused by *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*.

2. **Assess whether the proposed cause can plausibly produce the effect**
   - A place like **Asia** cannot biologically “cause” TB in the direct mechanistic sense; the *pathogen* causes TB.  
   - However, **being in (or living in) Asia** can be associated with **higher exposure risk** due to factors like TB prevalence, crowding, healthcare access, and socioeconomic conditions. So **Asia can be a proxy for risk context**, making the direction “Asia → TB” at least *plausible in an epidemiologic/associational sense*.

3. **Evaluate the reverse direction**
   - **TB cannot cause Asia**. A disease cannot cause the existence of a continent/region.

4. **Choose the more likely causal direction**
   - Between the two, **A** is far more plausible than **B**.

<Answer>A</Answer>
