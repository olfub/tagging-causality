Temporally, socioeconomic status is often established earlier in life (through education and career) and influences later purchases like cars, supporting the direction from SocioEcon to OtherCar.

In insurance risk modeling, socioeconomic factors are typically treated as causal predictors for many behavioral and ownership variables, including car ownership. The reverse direction would be unusual and less theoretically grounded.
