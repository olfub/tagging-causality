# Analyzing the Causal Relationship Between Alarm and MaryCalls

Let me analyze both potential causal directions:

## Option A: Alarm causes MaryCalls
This suggests that when an alarm is triggered, Mary makes a call in response.

This aligns with common scenarios:
- Mary could be a security guard who calls when security alarms go off
- Mary could be a family member who calls emergency services when a home alarm sounds
- Mary could be a monitoring service employee who calls homeowners when their alarm activates

## Option B: MaryCalls causes Alarm
This suggests that Mary making a call somehow triggers an alarm.

This is less plausible because:
- It's difficult to conceive of a standard mechanism where a person's call would trigger an alarm
- It would require an unusual system where outgoing calls activate alarm systems
- This reverses the typical information flow in alarm systems

## Conclusion
The most logical causal direction is that the alarm is the trigger that causes Mary to make a call. This follows the natural sequence where an event occurs (alarm), which prompts a human response (Mary calling).
