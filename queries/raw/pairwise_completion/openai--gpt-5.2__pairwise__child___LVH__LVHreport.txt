   - The **heart’s structure/function** exists first (LVH may or may not be present).
   - A **test/clinician interpretation** then observes and documents that finding, producing an **LVH report**.

3. **Check plausibility of the reverse direction**
   - A “report” cannot biologically cause myocardial hypertrophy. At most, it might influence downstream **management**, but it does not directly create LVH.

4. **Conclusion**
   - LVH is the underlying state that leads to the report describing it.
