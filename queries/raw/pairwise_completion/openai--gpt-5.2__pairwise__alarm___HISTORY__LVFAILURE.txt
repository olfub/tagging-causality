   - **HISTORY** typically refers to the patient’s *pre-existing medical history* (e.g., prior heart disease, hypertension, previous MI, cardiomyopathy, prior CHF, etc.). This is determined **before** the current ICU episode and is not caused by events occurring during the ICU stay.

2. **Check temporal ordering (a key causal clue)**
   - A patient’s **history** exists **prior to** developing (or decompensating into) LV failure in the current episode.
   - LV failure occurring now cannot retroactively create a prior medical history (though it may change what is documented going forward, it doesn’t cause the underlying past conditions).

3. **Clinical plausibility**
   - Many elements of medical **history** are well-known causes/risk factors for LV failure (ischemic heart disease, prior MI, valvular disease, longstanding hypertension, cardiomyopathies, etc.).
   - The reverse direction (“LV failure causes HISTORY”) is not physiologically meaningful as a causal claim; at most, LV failure could lead to *new documentation*, but that’s a documentation artifact, not HISTORY being caused.
