

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

**Understanding the variables:**

- **RiskAversion** is a personality trait or behavioral tendency describing how much a person seeks to avoid risk.
- **AntiTheft** refers to whether a vehicle has an anti-theft device installed.

**Evaluating Option A: AntiTheft causes RiskAversion**
- This would mean installing an anti-theft device *makes* a person risk-averse. This is implausible — a device installation doesn't reshape a person's fundamental attitude toward risk.

**Evaluating Option B: RiskAversion causes AntiTheft**
- This would mean a person who is naturally risk-averse is more likely to install an anti-theft device. This makes intuitive sense: risk-averse individuals take more precautionary measures (e.g., buying safety features, installing alarms, choosing comprehensive insurance). Their pre-existing personality trait *drives* the decision to install anti-theft protection.

**Conclusion:**
RiskAversion is a latent personality trait that influences observable behaviors like purchasing anti-theft devices. The causal arrow flows from the trait to the action, not the other way around.

<Answer>B</Answer>
