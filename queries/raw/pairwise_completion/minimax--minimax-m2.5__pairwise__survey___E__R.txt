   - Numerous studies on “neighbourhood effects” show that children growing up in low‑income or disadvantaged areas have lower educational attainment, even after controlling for family background.

   - For the majority of the life‑course, residence precedes formal education (children attend school where they live). This temporal ordering supports a causal role of residence on education.

   - The causal pathway **Residence → Education** is more consistently supported by empirical evidence and theory in demography.
