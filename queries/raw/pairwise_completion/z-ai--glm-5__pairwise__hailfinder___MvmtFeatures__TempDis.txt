# Analyzing Causal Direction Between MvmtFeatures and TempDis

## Understanding the Variables

First, I need to understand what these terms likely represent in hail forecasting:

- **MvmtFeatures**: Likely refers to "Movement Features" - characteristics of storm motion such as velocity, direction, and trajectory patterns
- **TempDis**: Likely refers to "Temperature Distribution" or "Temperature Disparity" - the spatial/temporal patterns of temperature in the atmosphere

## Causal Analysis

In meteorological systems, particularly for hail forecasting:

1. Temperature distributions in the atmosphere create pressure differentials
2. These pressure differentials drive wind patterns and atmospheric circulation
3. The resulting wind patterns influence how storms move and develop
4. Storm movement features (velocity, direction, etc.) emerge from these broader atmospheric dynamics

Temperature distributions are typically more fundamental drivers in the atmosphere. They create the conditions that determine how storms will move and evolve. Movement features of storms are more of an outcome of these atmospheric conditions.

## Less Likely Direction

It would be less plausible for storm movement features to cause temperature distributions at a fundamental level. While storms can locally modify temperatures, the overall temperature distribution in the atmosphere is primarily determined by larger scale factors.

## Conclusion

The more likely causal relationship is that temperature distributions (TempDis) cause the movement features (MvmtFeatures) of storms, rather than the reverse.
