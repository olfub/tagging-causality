2. **Assess whether the reverse direction is plausible:**  
   - Education does **not** make a person older or younger; it can correlate with age in datasets (older cohorts may have different schooling levels), but that’s not a direct causal effect of education on age.

3. **Interpretation in demographic terms:**  
   - Age is a fundamental demographic variable that often precedes and constrains education attainment (e.g., compulsory schooling ages, typical ages for college).  
   - Education can affect many outcomes (income, health, fertility), but **not age**.
