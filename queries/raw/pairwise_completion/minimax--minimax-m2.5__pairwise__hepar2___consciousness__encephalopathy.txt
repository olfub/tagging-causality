2. **Effect on brain function** – Because encephalopathy disrupts normal neuronal activity, it commonly produces a range of neurological symptoms such as confusion, memory problems, altered behavior, and changes in the level of consciousness.

3. **Consciousness as a brain‑dependent state** – Consciousness depends on intact cortical and subcortical networks. Any pathology that interferes with these networks (e.g., encephalopathy) can diminish or alter consciousness.

4. **Direction of causality** – The medical literature consistently shows that encephalopathic processes *cause* disturbances in consciousness (e.g., obtundation, stupor, coma). The reverse—consciousness causing brain disease—does not make physiological sense.
