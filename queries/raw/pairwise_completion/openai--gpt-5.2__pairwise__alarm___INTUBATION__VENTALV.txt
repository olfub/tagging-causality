In ICU physiology and workflow, **intubation** is an intervention that establishes a secure airway and typically enables **controlled/measured mechanical ventilation** (e.g., set tidal volume/minute ventilation/alveolar ventilation variables recorded by the ventilator).

Step by step:

1. **Temporal/clinical ordering:** Patients are usually **intubated first**, then placed on a ventilator with measurable ventilation settings and derived ventilation quantities.
2. **Data-generation mechanism:** Variables like **VENTALV** (a ventilation-related measurement/setting) are often **only present or become directly controllable/recorded once the patient is intubated and ventilated**.
3. **Alternative direction check:** While inadequate ventilation can *motivate* intubation clinically, the recorded ventilation variable itself does not usually *cause* the act of intubation; it’s more typically a downstream manifestation/measurement after airway control is established.
