**Step 1: Understanding Bilirubin**
Bilirubin is a yellow pigment produced during the normal breakdown of red blood cells (heme catabolism). It's processed by the liver and excreted in bile.

**Step 2: Understanding the Relationship with Skin**
When bilirubin levels become elevated in the blood (hyperbilirubinemia), it deposits in tissues including the skin, causing a yellow discoloration known as jaundice or icterus.

**Step 3: Evaluating Option A (bilirubin causes skin)**
While imprecisely worded, this captures the correct direction of causality. Elevated bilirubin *affects* the skin by causing yellow discoloration (jaundice). Bilirubin levels influence skin appearance.
