To determine the most likely cause-and-effect relationship between "PBC" and "joints," we need to define the terms and analyze the medical literature regarding their association.

**Step 1: Define the terms.**
*   **PBC (Primary Biliary Cholangitis):** Formerly known as Primary Biliary Cirrhosis, this is an autoimmune disease of the liver. It results in the slow destruction of the small bile ducts, causing bile to build up in the liver, leading to inflammation and scarring.
*   **Joints:** In this context, "joints" is shorthand for joint symptoms or joint pathology, such as arthralgia (joint pain) or arthritis (joint inflammation).

**Step 2: Analyze the relationship (A: PBC causes joints).**
*   PBC is a systemic autoimmune disease. Like many autoimmune disorders (such as Lupus or Rheumatoid Arthritis), it is frequently accompanied by extra-hepatic (outside the liver) manifestations.
*   Rheumatologic symptoms are very common in patients with PBC. Studies suggest that a significant percentage (often cited between 40-70%) of patients with PBC experience joint pain (arthralgia) or develop specific arthritis conditions associated with the autoimmune nature of the disease.
*   Therefore, the pathophysiology of PBC (the underlying autoimmune dysfunction) leads to the symptom of joint pain or inflammation.

**Step 3: Analyze the relationship (B: joints causes PBC).**
*   "Joints" (or joint pain/arthritis) are physical structures or symptoms. They do not have a biological mechanism to "cause" an autoimmune liver disease.
*   While having an existing autoimmune joint disease (like Rheumatoid Arthritis) is a risk factor for developing *another* autoimmune disease like PBC due to shared genetic susceptibility, the joints themselves do not cause the liver disease.
*   Therefore, stating that "joints cause PBC" is medically inaccurate causation.

**Step 4: Conclusion.**
The presence of Primary Biliary Cholangitis (PBC) leads to manifestations of joint complications (arthralgia/arthritis) due to the systemic nature of the autoimmune disorder. The reverse is not true; joints do not cause the liver disease.
