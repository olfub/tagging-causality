

## Step-by-step reasoning:

1. **Steatosis** (fatty liver disease) is a condition where excess fat accumulates in liver cells.

2. **Alcoholism** (chronic excessive alcohol consumption) is a behavioral/substance use disorder.

3. It is well-established in medical literature that chronic alcohol consumption disrupts normal liver metabolism — specifically, it impairs fatty acid oxidation, increases lipogenesis, and promotes fat accumulation in hepatocytes.

4. **Alcoholic steatosis** (alcoholic fatty liver) is recognized as the earliest and most common stage of **alcoholic liver disease (ALD)**. It can progress to steatohepatitis, fibrosis, and eventually cirrhosis.

5. The reverse direction — steatosis causing someone to develop alcoholism — has no established biological or behavioral mechanism.

6. Therefore, **alcoholism is the cause**, and **steatosis is the effect**.
