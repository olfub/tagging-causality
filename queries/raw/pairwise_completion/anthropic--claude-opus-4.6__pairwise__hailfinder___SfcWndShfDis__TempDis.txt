

# Analyzing the Causal Relationship Between SfcWndShfDis and TempDis

## Step-by-step reasoning:

1. **Identify the variables:**
   - **SfcWndShfDis**: Surface Wind Shear/Shift Discriminant — related to changes in surface wind direction/speed.
   - **TempDis**: Temperature Discriminant — related to temperature gradients or discontinuities.

2. **Fundamental atmospheric physics:**
   - Temperature gradients (baroclinicity) create pressure gradients via the hydrostatic and ideal gas relationships.
   - Pressure gradients drive wind, and horizontal temperature differences drive wind shear through the **thermal wind relationship**.
   - Surface wind shifts (e.g., across frontal boundaries) are largely a *consequence* of temperature (density) contrasts between air masses.

3. **In the hail forecasting context:**
   - Temperature differences between air masses establish the baroclinic zones and frontal boundaries.
   - These temperature contrasts create the pressure perturbations that manifest as surface wind shifts/shear.
   - While wind shifts can *advect* different temperature air masses to a point, the **root cause** of the wind shift is the pre-existing thermal gradient.

4. **Conclusion:**
   - Temperature differences are the more fundamental forcing mechanism that drives surface wind shifts, not the reverse.
