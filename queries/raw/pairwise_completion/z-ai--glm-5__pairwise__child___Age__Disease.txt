Here is the step-by-step reasoning to determine the correct cause-and-effect relationship:

**1. Define the Variables**
*   **Age:** This variable represents the length of time a person has lived (chronological age) or, in the context of risk factors, the age of the parents (maternal/paternal age).
*   **Disease:** In this context, Pediatric Congenital Heart Disease (CHD).

**2. Analyze Option B: Disease causes Age**
*   This direction implies that contracting the disease causes the patient to become a specific age or causes the passage of time.
*   Chronological age is a function of time passing, which is an independent variable. A disease cannot cause a person to be 5 years old or 10 years old; time passes regardless of health status.
*   While severe chronic disease can affect *biological* aging (phenotypic changes) or life expectancy (limiting the age one reaches), the fundamental arrow of time (Age) precedes the disease state.
*   Therefore, it is logically and scientifically incorrect to say Disease causes Age.

**3. Analyze Option A: Age causes Disease**
*   This direction implies that age is a risk factor or determinant for the disease.
*   **Maternal Age:** In the context of congenital heart disease, advanced maternal age is a well-established non-modifiable risk factor. Older maternal age is associated with a higher incidence of chromosomal abnormalities (such as Down syndrome) which are strongly linked to CHD. Thus, age acts as a cause (risk factor) for the occurrence of the disease.
*   **Patient Age:** While CHD is "congenital" (present at birth), the age of the patient determines the physiological expression of the disease. However, the strongest causal link in etiology is Maternal Age -> Disease.
*   **General Medical Principle:** In epidemiology, Age is consistently treated as an independent variable (a cause) that influences the probability and prevalence of a Disease (the effect).

**4. Compare the Likelihood**
*   Option B (Disease -> Age) violates the fundamental laws of physics (causality of time).
*   Option A (Age -> Disease) aligns with established medical risk factors (specifically maternal age as a risk factor for congenital anomalies).

**Conclusion**
Age is a determinant or risk factor for the disease, whereas the disease cannot cause the passage of time (Age).
