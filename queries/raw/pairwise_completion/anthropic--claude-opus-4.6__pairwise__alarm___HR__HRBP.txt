

# Causal Relationship Between HR and HRBP

## Step-by-Step Analysis

1. **Define the variables:**
   - **HR (Heart Rate):** The fundamental heart rate, typically measured via ECG (electrocardiogram), reflecting the heart's electrical activity.
   - **HRBP (Heart Rate from Blood Pressure):** The heart rate derived/estimated from the arterial blood pressure waveform.

2. **Physiological mechanism:**
   - The heart's electrical activity triggers mechanical contraction (systole).
   - Each contraction produces a pulse in the arterial blood pressure waveform.
   - HRBP is essentially *counted* from these pressure pulses.

3. **Causal direction:**
   - The true heart rate (HR) drives the cardiac contractions → which generate the arterial pressure waveform → from which HRBP is derived.
   - HRBP is a **downstream measurement** that depends on the underlying heart rate, not the other way around.

4. **ICU monitoring context:**
   - In the well-known ICU alarm network (e.g., the ALARM Bayesian network), HR is modeled as a parent node that causally influences HRBP, consistent with the physiological reasoning above.

## Conclusion

The true heart rate (HR) is the fundamental physiological variable that causes the blood pressure waveform to exhibit pulses, from which HRBP is derived. HR is the cause; HRBP is the effect.
