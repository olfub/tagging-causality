To determine the more likely cause-and-effect relationship between `hbc_anti` (Hepatitis B Core Antibody) and `hbeag` (Hepatitis B e-Antigen), we must analyze the biological and immunological mechanisms of Hepatitis B Virus (HBV) infection.

1.  **Identify the Nature of the Markers:**
    *   **hbeag (HBeAg):** This is a viral **antigen**. It is a soluble protein produced and secreted by the Hepatitis B virus during active replication. Its presence indicates high viral infectivity.
    *   **hbc_anti (Anti-HBc):** This is a host **antibody**. It is produced by the patient's immune system in response to the Hepatitis B Core Antigen (HBcAg). Its presence indicates current or past exposure to the virus.

2.  **Analyze the Immunological Causal Chain:**
    *   In the context of an infection, the pathogen (the virus) enters the body first.
    *   The virus replicates and produces viral proteins (antigens), such as HBeAg and HBcAg.
    *   The host's immune system recognizes these foreign antigens and responds by producing specific antibodies, such as Anti-HBc.
    *   Therefore, the fundamental biological direction is **Antigen (Virus) $\rightarrow$ Antibody (Immune Response)**.

3.  **Evaluate the Options:**
    *   **A. hbc_anti causes hbeag:** This would imply that the host's antibody production causes the virus to produce the e-antigen. Biologically, antibodies are a response to the infection, not a cause of viral protein production. In fact, the immune response aims to clear the antigen.
    *   **B. hbeag causes hbc_anti:** While technically the Core Antigen (HBcAg) directly stimulates Anti-HBc, HBeAg is a marker of active viral replication. High levels of viral replication (indicated by HBeAg) result in the presence of Core Antigens, which in turn stimulate the production of Anti-HBc. Thus, the state of active viral antigenemia (HBeAg) is causally upstream of the serological immune response (Anti-HBc) in the progression of the disease markers.

4.  **Conclusion:**
    Based on the principle that antigens stimulate the production of antibodies, the presence of the viral antigen marker (`hbeag`) is the cause (or upstream indicator of the cause) that leads to the effect of the antibody marker (`hbc_anti`).

<Answer>B</Answer>
