To determine which cause-and-effect relationship is more likely, let's analyze the medical definitions and pathophysiological links between the two terms.

**Step 1: Identify the terms.**
*   **Alcoholism:** This is a chronic disease characterized by uncontrolled drinking and preoccupation with alcohol. It is a behavioral and physiological disorder.
*   **THepatitis (Toxic Hepatitis):** Hepatitis refers to inflammation of the liver. The "T" likely stands for "Toxic" in this context, referring to liver inflammation caused by exposure to certain substances such as drugs, chemicals, or alcohol. Alcohol-induced hepatitis is a specific and common form of toxic hepatitis.

**Step 2: Analyze Relationship A (THepatitis causes alcoholism).**
*   This suggests that having an inflamed liver (caused by toxins) compels a person to develop an addiction to alcohol.
*   While chronic illness can sometimes lead to depression or coping mechanisms involving substance use, liver inflammation itself does not physiologically cause the addiction syndrome known as alcoholism. In fact, a diagnosis of hepatitis usually necessitates the *cessation* of alcohol use to prevent further damage. Therefore, this direction of causality is medically illogical as a primary mechanism.

**Step 3: Analyze Relationship B (alcoholism causes THepatitis).**
*   This suggests that the chronic, excessive consumption of alcohol leads to toxic liver inflammation.
*   The liver is the primary organ responsible for metabolizing alcohol. When alcohol is consumed in excess over time, the metabolic process produces toxic byproducts (like acetaldehyde) that damage liver cells, trigger inflammation, and lead to cell death.
*   This condition is clinically known as Alcoholic Hepatitis, which falls under the umbrella of Toxic Hepatitis. It is a well-established medical progression: Alcoholism $\rightarrow$ Toxic exposure to the liver $\rightarrow$ Hepatitis.

**Step 4: Conclusion.**
The direct physiological pathway flows from the consumption of the toxin (alcoholism) to the organ damage (hepatitis).

<Answer>B</Answer>
