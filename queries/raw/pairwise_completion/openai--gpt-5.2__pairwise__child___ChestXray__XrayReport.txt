1. **Define the variables**
   - **ChestXray**: the imaging study/procedure (the radiograph is taken).
   - **XrayReport**: the radiologist’s interpretation/documentation of findings from that imaging.

2. **Temporal order**
   - The **ChestXray must occur first** (image acquisition).
   - The **XrayReport is produced afterward** based on reviewing the images.

3. **Mechanism**
   - The report is **derived from** the chest X-ray images; without the image, there is nothing to interpret and report.
   - In clinical workflow, ordering/performing the chest X-ray **leads to** generating a report.

4. **Counterfactual check**
   - If no **ChestXray** is performed, typically no **XrayReport** exists.
   - Creating an **XrayReport** does not cause the chest X-ray to have been performed; at most, a *clinical suspicion/order* could lead to getting an X-ray, but that’s a different variable than the report itself.

Therefore, **ChestXray → XrayReport** is the more plausible cause-and-effect relationship.
