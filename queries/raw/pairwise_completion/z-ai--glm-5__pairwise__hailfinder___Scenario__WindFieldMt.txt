- They then apply various models and tools, including wind field models, to these scenarios

1. A scenario is defined with specific parameters and conditions
2. The WindFieldMt model is applied to that scenario
3. The model generates wind field predictions based on the scenario

Does WindFieldMt cause scenarios to exist? No. The wind field model is a tool applied within scenarios, not something that creates scenarios. Scenarios are typically defined by meteorologists based on historical weather patterns, forecasts, or hypothetical situations they want to analyze.
