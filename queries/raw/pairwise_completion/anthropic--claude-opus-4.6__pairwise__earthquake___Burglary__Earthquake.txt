

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

**Option A: Burglary causes Earthquake**
- A burglary is a human-scale criminal act (breaking into a building to steal).
- Earthquakes are massive geological events caused by tectonic plate movements, volcanic activity, or other large-scale geological processes.
- There is no plausible physical mechanism by which a burglary could trigger an earthquake.

**Option B: Earthquake causes Burglary**
- Earthquakes cause widespread destruction, power outages, and displacement of residents.
- Damaged or abandoned properties with broken security systems become vulnerable targets.
- Reduced law enforcement presence (redirected to rescue operations) creates opportunity.
- Post-earthquake looting and burglary is a well-documented phenomenon throughout history.

**Conclusion:** There is a clear and well-established causal pathway from earthquakes to increased burglary, while the reverse has no plausible mechanism.

<Answer>B</Answer>
