When a patient ingests or is exposed to a hepatotoxic substance (e.g., acetaminophen overdose, certain antibiotics, alcohol), the chemical injury to the liver cells triggers an inflammatory response. This specific type of inflammation is clinically defined as reactive hepatitis or drug-induced liver injury (DILI).

*   Hepatotoxicity is the mechanism of injury.
